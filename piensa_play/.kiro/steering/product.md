# PiensaPlay Product Overview

PiensaPlay is an educational mobile application designed to teach children about media literacy and digital safety through interactive games and activities.

## Target Audience
Children (primary users) with tutor/educator oversight

## Core Features
- User profile creation with customizable avatars
- Game-based learning units focused on digital safety
- Interactive glossary of media/safety terms
- Progress tracking system
- Tutor dashboard for content management
- Multi-language support (Spanish primary, prepared for expansion)

## User Roles
- **Students**: Children who play educational games and learn digital safety concepts
- **Tutors**: Educators who manage content, create game units, and monitor student progress

## Design Philosophy
- Colorful, child-friendly interface
- Gamified learning experience
- Safe, educational environment
- Responsive design for multiple devices
